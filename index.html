🤏 Labels not lining up? (print offset)

Some printers silently add a top/side margin. Print the test sheet once, measure the gap around the dashed boxes, then enter the millimetres here (negative = moves up/left). Saved on this device only.

How it works

  1. Download the CSV template (above) and add your pupils' names, classes and subjects.
  2. Drop the file here — your columns are mapped for you automatically.
  3. Pick your Avery sheet, preview, then print at 100% scale (or save as PDF).
