What can you use the label maker for?

Pupil names are just the start — teachers use the same free tool for all of these.

  • Exercise book labels

    Name, class and subject strips for the books that fill a locker and travel home every night.

  • Subject labels

    Maths, English, Science, PE and every other subject — colour-coded straight from a CSV column.

  • Folder labels

    Ring binders, display folders and topic folders for pupils, classes and staff.

  • Chromebook & laptop labels

    Asset tags for devices, chargers, cases and trolleys, with the class or year group on them.

  • Library book labels

    Name stickers for class sets, reading corners and shelves that need to travel back to the right child.

  • Classroom equipment labels

    Whiteboard pens, iPads, counters, lab glassware and PE kit that walk off if you let them.

  • Pupil name labels

    Coat pegs, trays, drawers, desks and lunchboxes — the start-of-year name blitz in one print run.

  • Staff equipment labels

    Laptops, keys, mugs and shared kit in the staffroom, office and prep room.

  • Storage box labels

    Resource boxes, trays and stackable crates in classrooms, cupboards and stock rooms.

  • Stationery labels

    Pencil cases, scissors, glue sticks, rulers and every other item that gets left behind.

Same steps for any of these: add your list, check the preview, print at 100%. Nothing you add is uploaded anywhere.

Your pupil data stays private

Nothing you paste, type or upload is sent anywhere. The label maker runs entirely in the browser on your own device — there is no account, no upload, and no online storage of pupil names. Open the page, make your labels, and close it: the data goes with you.

Printing your labels

Labels are sized for A4 Avery sheets and should be printed at 100% scale with automatic scaling turned off and background graphics switched on, so the colours and alignment guides print accurately. You can also save the sheet as a PDF.
